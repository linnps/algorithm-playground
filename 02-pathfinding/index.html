Planned interactive demo

A grid you can sketch walls onto with click-and-drag. Place a start and goal, then watch A*, Dijkstra, BFS, and DFS run side-by-side, painting visited cells in shades of blue and the final shortest path in red.

What it teaches

  • Why A*'s heuristic-guided search reaches the goal with fewer expanded cells than Dijkstra.
  • Why BFS finds shortest paths on unweighted grids but loses optimality the moment you introduce edge weights.
  • Why DFS is *terrible* at finding shortest paths — even though it always finds *a* path eventually.
  • What happens to A* as you swap Manhattan distance for Euclidean, Chebyshev, or no heuristic at all.
