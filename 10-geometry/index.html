Planned interactive demo

Click anywhere on the canvas to drop points. The Graham-scan convex hull recomputes live, with the angular-sort step animated and the cross-product test highlighting which candidate edges get popped from the stack.

What it teaches

  • The convex hull is the simplest non-trivial computational-geometry problem and a gateway to sweep-line techniques.
  • Why the cross-product test (rather than angle comparisons) is the right primitive — it's exact in integer arithmetic and avoids floating-point drift.
  • Graham scan's O(n log n) is dominated by the angular sort; the hull-construction sweep itself is O(n).
