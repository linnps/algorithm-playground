Planned interactive demo

A row of buckets, three side-by-side panels — one per collision-resolution strategy. Insert keys and watch each strategy distribute them differently. A load-factor slider shows how each strategy degrades as the table fills: chaining stays graceful, linear probing develops "clusters," cuckoo hashing eventually fails to insert and triggers a rehash.

What it teaches

  • Why a "good" hash function matters more than people think — bad hashes turn linear probing into linked lists.
  • The clustering effect: linear probing's cost grows non-linearly past ~70% load factor.
  • Cuckoo hashing's elegant guarantee — O(1) lookup worst case, even under collisions, at the cost of O(amortized) insertions.
